Most agencies ask you to trust the number. We would rather show you the account it came from.

The roofing niche is full of screenshots with no source, ROI multiples that can't be reproduced, and lead counts that quietly round up. We built this business on the opposite bet: publish only what a dashboard can prove, name the date range, and hand over the login on the first call.

It's a slower way to grow an agency. It's also the only version that survives a roofer actually checking.

Questions

Frequently asked

What does Roofers Growths actually do?

We are a marketing agency working with roofing contractors and nobody else. SEO, Google Ads, websites, local search and lead systems, built around how roofing actually sells.

Are the numbers on this site real?

Yes, and you can check them. Every figure comes from a GoHighLevel pipeline we still run, the date range is stated, and the dashboard sits on the case study page. We removed every claim we could not evidence, which is why our numbers are smaller than most agencies'.

How much does it cost?

Engagements start at $599 a month. We publish the ranges rather than hiding them, because a roofer who cannot afford us should find that out in thirty seconds, not at the end of a sales call.

How long before I see results?

Paid search can produce leads within days. SEO takes three to six months for meaningful movement and six to nine before it is a channel you would rely on. The real timelines are in every case study.

Will you work with my competitor?

No. One roofing company per market. If we already work with someone in your service area we will tell you immediately and refer you elsewhere.
