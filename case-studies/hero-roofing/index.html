What "opportunity" means

A qualified lead entered into the client's CRM pipeline. Not a page view, not an impression, not a bot form fill. See how we count.

The problem

Hero Roofing was competing in a crowded market with lead flow that spiked after storms and collapsed in between. No predictable cost per job.

What we did

  • Rebuilt the service pages around replacement and repair intent rather than generic roofing keywords.
  • Restructured the Google Business Profile and built review velocity from completed jobs.
  • Installed call tracking so every lead traced back to the search that produced it.

What did not work

Before bringing in Roofers Growths, we recognized significant gaps and holes in our previous marketing strategy. Monitoring budgets and managing the day-to-day details of our digital space became too difficult to control internally, leaving us without clear tracking on what our leads actually cost. We needed a dedicated partner to take over so we could stop guessing and start seeing real, measurable growth.

The result

243 opportunities and 19 closed contracts worth $155,150 in six months. The close rate is 7.8%, lower than we want it, and the next phase of this account is intake, not more traffic.
