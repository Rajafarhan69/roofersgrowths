The honest version

Why cheap SEO does not work

Roofing SEO is not a product with a marginal cost of zero. Every month it takes real hours: technical work, content, local optimisation, link acquisition, reporting. At a low enough price an agency has two options: do almost nothing and hope you do not notice, or automate it into spam that eventually damages your site.

Both happen constantly, which is why so many roofers believe SEO does not work. It works. What does not work is buying it for less than it costs to do.

What you should compare us against

Not a cheaper agency. The leads you are buying right now. Take what you pay per shared lead, divide by your close rate, and you have your real cost per booked job. Then compare that against a retainer that builds an asset you own.

For reference: across our accounts this year the average signed contract is $13,773 and the blended close rate is 10.6%. Run that maths on your own numbers before you speak to anybody, including us.

Questions

Frequently asked

Why is there no cheap starter plan?

Because it would not work, and selling something that cannot work is how this industry got its reputation. Real roofing SEO takes content, technical work, local optimisation and link acquisition every month. Below roughly $599 none of that can be funded, so the client pays for twelve months and gets nothing.

Is there a contract?

No. You can end the engagement at any time, with no restrictions and no lock-in. You keep everything we've built either way.

What happens if I leave?

You keep everything: the website, the content, the ad accounts, the CRM, the tracking, the data, the domain. We do not hold assets hostage.

Do you take a percentage of ad spend?

No. That would give us a financial incentive to spend more of your money. We charge a flat fee.

Can I start with just an audit?

Yes, and it is free. Twenty minutes, recorded, covering your site, your Google Business Profile and your three closest competitors. You keep the recording whether or not you hire us.
