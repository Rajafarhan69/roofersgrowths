Locations

Roofing marketing, by what actually drives your market

Hail, hurricanes, and storm-chaser competition are three different problems. We do not run the same playbook in every metro, and these pages show exactly what changes.

TX

Houston

A hail-and-hurricane insurance-restoration market with national franchises and established locals competing for the same claims. Speed to lead and honest GBP service areas matter more here than almost anywhere.

TX

Dallas-Fort Worth

North Texas hail hits in narrow, specific swaths, and every event draws traveling storm-chaser crews. The real competitor is not another local roofer, it is a crew that will be gone in six weeks.

FL

Miami

Hurricane season runs on a predictable calendar, and Florida's insurance crisis forces roof-age replacements independent of storm damage. Tile roofing and a bilingual market change the whole sales conversation.

MO/KS

Kansas City

The one metro that spans two states, with different licensing rules on each side. Storms bring hail and wind together, concentrated in a narrow spring season.

Oklahoma City

Tornado-driven catastrophic loss, not routine hail claims. Same-day emergency tarping decides who gets the much larger rebuild job later.
