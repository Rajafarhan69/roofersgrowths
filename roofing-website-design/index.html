Website design

Roofing website design

A roofing website has one job: get a worried homeowner to call you instead of the next company on the list.

Book a free audit

Overview

Most roofing websites are built to impress the owner. This one is built to convert his customer: on a phone, on cellular data, in a driveway, in the rain, looking at a damaged roof and picking from three search results.

What matters on a roofing website

Speed, phone number, proof, and a single obvious next action. In that order. Everything else, the animations, the sliders, the stock photos of smiling families, is decoration that costs you load time and gains you nothing. We have never seen a slider increase a roofing lead rate.

What we build

01

Built for mobile first

Because that is where roofing searches happen, usually outdoors, usually on cellular.

02

Fast

Core Web Vitals in the green. Every second of load time costs you leads, and Google knows it.

03

Proof above the fold

Real photos of real jobs and real reviews. Not stock imagery of a roof that is not yours.

04

One clear action

Call, or book an inspection. Competing calls-to-action halve each other.

05

Built for search from day one

Structure, schema, headings and service architecture designed to rank, not retrofitted later.

06

Yours to keep

You own the site, the content, the hosting and the domain. Always.

What we do not do

We do not build sites you cannot edit. We do not host your site somewhere you cannot access. We do not withhold the files. When the engagement ends, you keep everything, because a website you do not control is a liability, not an asset.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need a new website, or can you fix mine?

Often we can fix it, and that is cheaper for you, so we will say so. A rebuild is worth it when the site is slow, structurally broken, or built in a way that fights search. We will tell you which after an audit.

How long does a roofing website take?

Typically four to eight weeks depending on how much content and photography needs producing. Rushing it is how you end up rebuilding it in a year.
