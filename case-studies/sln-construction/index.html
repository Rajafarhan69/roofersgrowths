What "opportunity" means

A qualified lead entered into the client's CRM pipeline. Not a page view, not an impression, not a bot form fill. See how we count.

The problem

SLN was ranking on page 16, effectively invisible, and carrying the whole company on paid channels.

What we did

  • Fixed the technical foundation that was stopping the site ranking at all.
  • Rebuilt the page structure so each service targeted a single search intent.
  • Ran Google Ads alongside SEO to hold lead flow steady while organic climbed.

What did not work

Before discovering Roofers Growths, we churned through several expensive marketing agencies, paying up to $5,000 a month for empty promises and confusing tech jargon. These past companies were incredibly hard to get in touch with, and despite the high costs, we couldn't attribute a single phone call or lead to their efforts. We desperately needed a transparent partner who actually communicated clearly and could deliver the tangible, organic growth they promised instead of just taking our money.

The result

305 opportunities and 19 closed contracts worth $247,520 so far this year, with the site moving from page 16 to page 1 for its core terms.
