Overview

Most roofing SEO fails for the same reason: the agency optimises for traffic that will never call. We build the opposite: fewer keywords, higher intent, and tracking that shows which search produced which job.

What roofing SEO actually is

Roofing SEO is the process of making a roofing company's website, Google Business Profile and service pages rank for the searches homeowners run when they are ready to hire. It is not blog volume, and it is not keyword density. It is intent capture. A homeowner searching "roof leak repair near me" at 9pm is worth more than a hundred readers of "10 types of shingles", and the whole system should be built around that fact.

What we do

01

Technical foundation

Crawlability, indexation, site speed, Core Web Vitals, structured data. If Google cannot read the site properly, nothing else matters.

02

Service page architecture

One page per service, per intent. Roof replacement, repair, inspection, storm damage, commercial: each targeting the searches that actually produce that job.

03

Local and Maps visibility

Google Business Profile, service-area setup, review velocity, and the local signals that decide who appears in the 3-pack.

04

Content that answers real questions

The questions homeowners ask before they call: cost, insurance, timelines, materials. Written to be useful, not to hit a word count.

05

Lead tracking

Call tracking and form attribution, so every lead is traceable to the keyword that produced it. If we cannot measure it, we will not claim it.

06

Reporting you can check

Monthly reporting in plain language, tied to leads and jobs. Access to every dashboard. No black boxes.

Who this is for

Roofing companies doing roughly $1M to $20M a year who already do good work, already close leads when they get them, and are tired of buying them. If your close rate is broken, SEO will just deliver you more leads to lose. We will tell you that before we take your money.

How long it takes

Meaningful movement in three to six months. Lead flow you would call a channel in six to nine. Anyone in this industry promising page one in thirty days is either lying to you or about to get your site penalised. We publish our timelines in every case study, including the slow ones.

Questions

Frequently asked

How much does roofing SEO cost?

Real roofing SEO in a competitive US market runs from roughly $599 to $1,199 a month depending on your market size, how many services you sell, and the state of your existing site. Anything materially below that range cannot fund the work required, which is why cheap SEO does not work rather than merely working slowly.

How long does roofing SEO take to work?

Three to six months for meaningful ranking movement, six to nine for lead flow you would call a channel, twelve or more to compete for the most valuable head terms in a large metro. It compounds: the twelfth month is worth far more than the third.

Is SEO better than Google Ads for roofers?

They do different jobs. Ads buy you leads today and stop the moment you stop paying. SEO builds an asset that produces leads at a declining marginal cost. Most roofers should run ads for cash flow while SEO compounds underneath. If you can only afford one and you need work this month, run ads. We will say so.

Do you guarantee rankings?

No, and neither can anyone else honestly. Nobody controls Google's algorithm. What we do guarantee is transparency: you see every dashboard, every report and every number, and you can cancel.
