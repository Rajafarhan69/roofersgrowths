AI search

AI search optimisation for roofers

Homeowners now ask ChatGPT which roofer to call. Almost no roofing company is optimised for that answer.

Overview

A growing share of buying research never touches a blue link. The homeowner asks an assistant, gets three recommendations, and calls one. If your company is not in that answer, you are not in the consideration set, and you will never see it in your analytics, because there is no click to measure.

How AI actually decides who to recommend

Not the way Google ranks. Language models assemble answers from what the wider web says about you: review platforms, directories, local press, forum threads, and structured, factual pages that are easy to extract. Your own website matters, but mostly as confirmation. The recommendation itself is earned off-site, in the places where other people talk about you.

What we do

01

Prompt tracking

A fixed set of buyer prompts, run monthly across every major assistant. Your scoreboard, and you see it.

02

Entity foundation

One consistent business identity across every platform, so the models can resolve who you are.

03

Extractable content

Answer-first structure, plain factual statements, definitions and tables: the format models actually quote.

04

Structured data

Organisation, Person, Service, FAQ and Review schema, so machines can read your business without guessing.

05

Source placement

Getting you into the directories, review platforms and articles that the models actually cite.

06

Crawler access

Making sure GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot and Google-Extended are not blocked. A surprising number of sites block them by accident and wonder why they are invisible.

The honest part

This is a young discipline and anyone claiming a proven playbook is selling you certainty they do not have. What we do is measurable: we track a fixed set of buyer prompts across ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity and AI Overviews every month, log who gets named and which sources get cited, and work to get you into those sources. You see the log.
