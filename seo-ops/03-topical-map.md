## Format guardrails (applies to every post)

- Real question as the H1 or close to it — matches how both Google's featured snippets and AI answer engines extract content.
- No fabricated statistics. If a stat is needed and no verified source exists, either cite Roofers Growths' own case-study data (already real) or skip the claim.
- Every post links to its pillar service page, at least one relevant case study, and one related post once one exists.
- FAQPage schema only where the FAQ content is genuinely useful, not bolted on for schema's sake.
