What "opportunity" means

A qualified lead entered into the client's CRM pipeline. Not a page view, not an impression, not a bot form fill. See how we count.

The problem

A newer roofer with no brand recognition, competing against companies that had been in the market for decades.

What we did

  • Built search visibility ahead of the phone call, so homeowners recognised the name at the point of comparison.
  • Targeted the full replacement journey rather than only bottom-of-funnel repair terms.
  • Produced content that established authority before the first conversation.

What did not work

Before working with Roofers Growths, my indirect experience with other marketing agencies left a lot to be desired. They completely lacked the personal communication and hands-on touch that a growing business needs. It was hard to build trust or feel confident that they truly understood our goals, which made scaling our brand visibility a frustrating and impersonal process.

The result

116 opportunities and 11 closed contracts worth $116,320 across seven months, at a 9.5% close rate, well above average for a brand this young.
