Google's AI Mode is now recommending Roofers Growths

A screenshot, dated, of what Google's AI Mode says when you ask it who the best marketing agency for roofers is. Here is what it shows, and what it does and does not prove.

By Rafero · Founder, Roofers Growths · August 28, 2026

In this piece

On August 28, 2026, we searched "best marketing agency for roofers in United States" in Google's AI Mode. This is what it returned, unedited, in a screenshot taken the same day.

What it actually says

The AI Mode answer opens by naming us directly: "Roofers Growths (often searched as Roofers Growth or Roofers Growth) is a specialized digital marketing agency that helps roofing contractors grow their businesses by finding more customers. Instead of working with all types of businesses, they focus entirely on the roofing industry." It goes on to describe our actual service lines, roofing SEO, local SEO and Google Maps, lead generation, and paid advertising, then cites roofersgrowths.com directly in the source panel alongside a separate roofing-marketing YouTube channel.

What this proves, and what it does not

It proves the site is structured in a way Google's AI systems can parse, extract, and quote directly, which is the entire premise of AI search optimisation as a discipline: clear entity definitions, direct answers, and content organised around what someone would actually ask, not just what ranks for a keyword. It is a real, dated result, and we would rather show you the raw screenshot than describe it secondhand.

It does not prove this is permanent. AI Mode answers are not static; the same query can return a different result next month, and we make no claim that this citation is guaranteed to persist. We are publishing it because it happened and we can show our work, the same standard we hold every case study on this site to, not because one snapshot means the work is finished.
