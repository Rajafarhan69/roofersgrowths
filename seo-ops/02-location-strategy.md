## Rule for every location page (from the master prompt, non-negotiable)

Each page needs genuinely different content — not a template with the city name swapped. Required per page:
- That market's actual driver (hail vs. hurricane vs. population growth/reroofing cycle — these are different sales stories)
- Realistic competitive density for a roofing marketing agency in that metro
- Local search behavior note (e.g. storm-market homeowners search reactively right after a weather event vs. growth-market homeowners searching on a longer replacement-cycle timeline)
- GBP/Maps strategy angle specific to whether the client base there is doing insurance-claim work or retail replacement
- Location-specific FAQ (not reused across cities)

## Do not build yet

No location page should go live until: (a) the two primary keyword targets are confirmed, and (b) at least one Tier 1 city has enough unique real detail to clear the "genuinely different" bar above. One well-built city page beats four templated ones.
