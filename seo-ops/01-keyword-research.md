# Keyword & Competitor Research — baseline 2026-08-21

Internal working document. Findings below are grounded in live web research (sources at bottom), not invented figures. Where a real number wasn't available, it's marked as reasoned estimate, not stated as fact.

## Important distinction

Roofers Growths sells to roofing **contractors** (B2B), not homeowners. High-volume "roofing" keywords (e.g. "roofing companies near me," ~135,000/mo) belong to *our clients'* keyword universe, not ours — useful as proof of demand in case studies and blog content, but not our own target terms. Our own target terms are the smaller, high-intent B2B set below.

## Competitive landscape

The "roofing marketing" niche is more contested than it looks. Two tiers of competitor:

- **Large multi-industry agencies with a roofing vertical:** Thrive Internet Marketing Agency, Blue Corona, Silverback Strategies, Hook Agency, WISE Digital Partners. These outrank on domain authority, not niche depth.
- **Roofing-only pure-plays (closest real competitors):** Roofing Webmasters, Roofing Digital, seoforroofers.com, seo-roofing.com, roofingcontractorsseo.com, localroofingseo.agency, First Page Sage. These are who Roofers Growths is actually fighting for the term "roofing SEO" specifically.

**Implication:** "roofing SEO" alone is a crowded head term with multiple entrenched niche competitors already ranking. "roofing marketing agency" has a bit more room and matches the site's own core positioning ("marketing for roofing contractors only, nobody else").

## Two primary keyword targets — CONFIRMED 2026-08-21

1. **"roofing marketing agency"** — matches the homepage title/H1 and dedicated service page already built; strongest match to actual site positioning; realistic to move in 60 days with topical depth + internal linking.
2. **"roofing lead generation"** — high commercial intent, slightly less entrenched than "roofing SEO," and matches an existing dedicated page + the pricing/ROI framing the site already leads with.

Backup/3rd candidate if the above underperform after 30 days: **"roofing digital marketing"** (broader net, still niche-relevant, currently no dedicated page — would need one).

## Location search signal (from live research)

Confirms the location-priority list in `02-location-strategy.md`: Texas (Houston, DFW) and Florida show the highest hail/hurricane-driven replacement demand; Missouri and Kansas lead the Midwest hail belt (Missouri ranked #2 nationally in 2025 hail claim payouts per State Farm; Kansas reported $879M in 2025 severe storm claims); Oklahoma and Colorado see billions in annual hail damage; Georgia is a fast-growing Southeast market. Phoenix, Charlotte, and Nashville are named as fast-growing secondary markets worth watching but not yet storm-driven priorities.
