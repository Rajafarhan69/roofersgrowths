Overview

Generalist agencies have to learn your business on your budget. We already know what a supplement is, why storm season breaks your lead flow, what a retail roofer needs that a restoration roofer does not, and why the lead that comes in at 4pm on a Friday is worth less than the one that comes in at 9am on a Tuesday.

Why roofing only

Because roofing does not market like other trades. Demand is weather-driven and spiky. Half the industry sells through insurance and half sells retail, and they need opposite funnels. Ticket sizes are high enough that one extra job pays for a month of marketing, which changes the entire economics of a campaign. An agency that also does dentists and law firms is guessing at all of this. We are not.

What we will tell you before you hire us

If your close rate is the problem, marketing will make it worse, not better. If you have no capacity to take on more work, more leads will damage your reputation. If your reviews are bad, no amount of visibility will save you. We will say all of this on the first call, because we would rather lose the sale than take on an engagement that cannot work. If you want the specific questions to ask any agency, including us, before you sign, we wrote them up in how to vet a roofing marketing agency.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do you work with roofing companies only?

Yes. Every client is a roofing contractor. We do not take home services generally, we do not take other trades, and we do not take work outside roofing even when it is offered.

Will you work with my competitor?

No. We work with one roofing company per market. If we are already working with someone in your service area, we will tell you on the first call and refer you elsewhere.

Do I own everything you build?

Yes. Your website, your content, your ad accounts, your tracking, your data. All of it stays yours, and you keep it if you leave. Agencies that hold your site hostage are the reason this industry has a trust problem.
